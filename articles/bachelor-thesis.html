Bachelor Thesis

Supervised by Prof. Tilman Plehn

Generating Z-Boson Events with Conditional Flow Matching and Consistency Models

This thesis explores the application of generative models in particle physics, specifically using Flow Matching and Consistency Models to simulate Z-boson events.

The goal was to improve simulation speed without sacrificing accuracy compared to traditional Monte Carlo methods.

Key Technologies

  • PyTorch / Python
  • Generative Models (Flow Matching)
  • High Energy Physics Simulation
